# Work Execution and Orchestration System

## Overview
A human–AI coordination layer and agent-native workflow engine for real-time execution and orchestration of knowledge work. Designed for engineering teams (10–50+ members). Integrates with management, issue tracking, Git, CI/CD pipelines, testing, and OpSec infrastructure. Collaboration is central to its design.

“What Jira is to intent, and CI/CD is to execution, this is to end-to-end software delivery with AI agents.”

## Target Users
- Engineering teams managing complex projects.
- Team leads, DevOps, and QA professionals.

## Key Features
- **Ingestion**: Captures and refines requirements. **Role**: Planning agents.
- **Clarification**: Resolves ambiguities. **Role**: Verification agents.
- **Planning**: Breaks down tasks. **Role**: Planning agents.
- **Execution**: Generates code. **Role**: Code-generation agents.
- **Verification**: Automates testing. **Role**: Test agents.
- **Scheduling**: Manages priorities and releases. **Role**: Scheduling agents.
- **Review**: Ensures all tasks are completed and validated. **Role**: Verification agents.

## Integration Requirements
Integrates with:
- **Project Management**: Jira, Trello, Asana.
- **Version Control**: Git, GitHub, GitLab.
- **CI/CD**: Jenkins, GitHub Actions.
- **Testing**: Selenium, Cypress.
- **Communication**: Slack.
- **Monitoring**: Prometheus, Grafana.

## Notification Strategy
- **AI Agents**: Real-time alerts through internal messaging protocols and dashboards for progress.
- **Stakeholders**: Email summaries, project tracking, regular syncs.

## Do's and Don'ts
### Do's
- Streamline workflows and integrate tools.
- Leverage AI for repetitive tasks.
- Foster collaboration across teams.

### Don'ts
- Replace project management tools.
- Ignore human oversight.
